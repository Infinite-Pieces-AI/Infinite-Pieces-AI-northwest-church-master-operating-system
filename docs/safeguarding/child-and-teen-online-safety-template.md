# Child and Teen Online Safety Policy Template

- Children under 13 have no independent account.
- Guardians control child profiles, pickup permissions, and media consent.
- Teen accounts require approved policy and guardian/leadership workflow.
- Unrestricted adult-to-teen direct messaging is disabled.
- Teen communication occurs in approved group channels with visible trained leader oversight.
- No public child directory, school, home address, precise location, or recurring availability.
- Safety reports are restricted and do not replace emergency or legally required reporting.
- All leaders receive training on boundaries, escalation, documentation, and off-platform emergencies.
- Access is removed immediately when a volunteer assignment ends.

Leadership and counsel must adapt this template to current church policy and law.
