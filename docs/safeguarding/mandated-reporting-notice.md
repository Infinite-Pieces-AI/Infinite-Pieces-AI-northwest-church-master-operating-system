# Safeguarding Reporting Notice

The in-app safeguarding pathway is a restricted documentation and escalation aid. It is not emergency services and is not a substitute for any immediate oral, written, or other reporting required by current law or church policy.

Before launch, qualified Massachusetts counsel and the church safeguarding authority must insert the current contacts, timing, responsible roles, after-hours procedure, preservation rules, and training acknowledgement. The application must display emergency instructions prominently and avoid suggesting that submitting a form completes a legal duty.
