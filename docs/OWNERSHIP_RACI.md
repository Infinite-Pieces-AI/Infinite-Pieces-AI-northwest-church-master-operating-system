# Ownership and RACI

| Responsibility             | Accountable owner                           | Required backup               |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Product authority          | Lowell/Northwest lead minister or appointee | Central church representative |
| Central coordination       | Central Boston Church representative        | Product owner                 |
| Technical architecture     | Experienced technical lead                  | Named backup engineer         |
| Security/incident response | Security owner                              | Secondary incident lead       |
| Kids safeguarding          | Children’s ministry safeguarding authority  | Trained deputy                |
| Privacy/records            | Administrative or legal/privacy owner       | Secondary records owner       |
| Content accuracy           | Ministerial content owner                   | Approved publisher            |
| Accessibility/UX           | Design/frontend owner                       | QA owner                      |
| Moderation                 | Moderation lead                             | Escalation moderator          |
| Outreach/analytics         | Communications lead                         | Approved publisher            |
| Sunday operations          | Check-in coordinator                        | Manual fallback lead          |

The church owns all production accounts and payment methods. Each vendor record contains primary and secondary recovery owners. Super-admin access is emergency-only and reviewed at least quarterly.
