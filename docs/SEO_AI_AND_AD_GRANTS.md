# SEO AI, Local Presence, Campaigns, and Ad Grants

## Responsible meaning of “finding people”

The platform may observe aggregate public search demand, publish useful local information, run geographically scoped contextual campaigns, invite voluntary contact, and measure aggregate conversions. It cannot reveal or infer the identity of a person who privately searched for God, prayer, belief, or church.

## Search-performance pipeline

```text
Search Console aggregate rows
→ sanitize query/page/date/click/impression/position
→ score opportunity
→ review existing-page improvement or people-first brief
→ combine only with approved church facts
→ communications review
→ theological review when applicable
→ publish through normal content workflow
→ measure meaningful public conversions
```

No member directory, prayer activity, private Bible questions, pastoral counseling, child records, or private-group activity may become an audience signal.

## People-first content studio

The studio can draft:

- Lowell church and Sunday-service guidance;
- what-to-expect and directions content;
- family, Kids Kingdom, teen, Bible-study, and family-group pages;
- event descriptions and unique event pages;
- sermon summaries and transcripts;
- social captions, email notices, short-video scripts, image prompts, alt text, and translations.

Every brief requires approved facts and substantive sections. The system does not mass-create thin ZIP-code pages, auto-publish generated text, or invent services, counseling, hours, ownership, or facility access.

## Local profile readiness

Because the church may meet in a rented/shared venue, the administrative checklist tracks:

- central approval of the official public identity;
- accurate venue representation and operating evidence;
- actual staffed service hours;
- documented signage and welcome-desk operation;
- accurate category selection;
- church-controlled account ownership and recovery.

The software records readiness and evidence; it does not guarantee eligibility or verification and must not misrepresent permanent occupancy.

## Campaign Studio

A campaign record contains:

- objective;
- approved geography;
- specific high-intent keyword groups;
- landing page;
- approved creative/version;
- UTM convention;
- optional budget;
- dates and status;
- meaningful conversion definitions;
- owner and approver;
- aggregate results.

Prohibited practices include member-list targeting, inferred religious-belief audiences, prayer-page retargeting, private-data lookalikes, and autonomous budget or publication changes.

## Ad Grants evaluation

The platform includes an evaluation checklist for organizational eligibility, church-controlled domain/email, substantive mission content, meaningful conversion tracking, keyword review, and advertising policy. Program eligibility, terms, verification partners, and performance rules can change; the accountable owner must verify current official requirements before applying or launching.

## Visitor CRM

Only voluntarily submitted contacts enter the CRM. Supported stages include new request, contact attempted, conversation established, visit scheduled, attended, Bible study requested, family group requested, member access requested, closed, and opted out.

Prayer text, private theological questions, child information, ministry assignments, and private messages are not exported to advertising platforms.
