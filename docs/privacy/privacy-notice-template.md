# Privacy Notice Template

> Draft for review by church leadership and qualified counsel. Replace brackets and approve before production.

Describe the church entity, contact, data categories, purposes, lawful/organizational basis, vendors, retention, security, guardian rights, access/correction/deletion process, media scopes, analytics separation, AI use, cross-border processing if any, incident contact, and effective date.

Use plain language. Do not claim guaranteed security, legal compliance, or confidentiality. Identify that emergency/safeguarding communications may require use of phone, emergency services, or legally required channels rather than the app.
