# AI Use and Approval Policy

## Allowed source classes

- approved belief documents
- published sermon transcripts
- published weekly lessons
- minister-approved study resources
- licensed Scripture passages/references
- public ministry FAQs

## Prohibited source classes by default

- child and guardian records
- check-in, attendance, pickup, care, or custody information
- prayer requests
- pastoral/counseling notes
- safeguarding and security incidents
- private direct/community messages unless a separately approved moderation workflow explicitly projects content
- credentials, tokens, secrets, or audit metadata

## Output presentation

Every Bible companion result visibly separates:

1. **Scripture** — exact licensed text/reference.
2. **Church teaching** — approved authored content.
3. **AI explanation** — generated material requiring discernment.

Every factual/theological claim derived from the knowledge base includes a source citation.

## Human approval

AI may draft summaries, questions, event copy, captions, translation, alt text, video scripts, and image prompts. A trained human approves before publication. Theological outputs require ministerial review. Database policy prevents a content editor from moving sermon curriculum into `approved` or `published`; only an MFA-authenticated minister or super administrator may do so.

## Prohibited authority

AI may not publish, send messages, communicate independently with minors, change access, assign groups, decide safeguarding reports, diagnose conditions, promise outcomes, or access database administrator credentials.

## Evaluation

Maintain a test set for citation accuracy, unsupported claims, doctrinal source separation, privacy leakage, prompt injection, minor-contact attempts, crisis/safeguarding prompts, and refusal quality. Record model/provider/version and re-run before material upgrades.

## Curriculum and creative drafts

A sermon-outline workflow may produce a concise weekly summary, seven-day reflection plan, small-group questions, parent discussion prompts, related Scripture references, captions, translations, short-video concepts, alt text, and image-generation prompts. Each output remains a versioned draft linked to the approved source documents and weekly lesson.

Generated people must be identified as fictional and may not be presented as photographs of actual members. Real children or real congregation media require the normal consent and media workflow.

## Search and outreach AI

AI can transform aggregate Search Console opportunities into reviewable people-first briefs. It may not identify a searcher, infer an individual’s religious beliefs, create private-data audiences, auto-publish thin local pages, or invent venue, schedule, counseling, ministry, or eligibility claims.
