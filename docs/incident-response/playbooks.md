# Incident Playbooks

## Lost privileged device
Revoke sessions, reset factors, review audit events, rotate exposed tokens, confirm no persistent access.

## Public/private data exposure
Disable affected route/policy, preserve logs, identify exact rows and viewers, involve privacy/legal owner, test fix against abuse case.

## Child-media exposure
Remove access immediately, preserve evidence privately, notify safeguarding/privacy leadership, execute external takedown checklist, review consent and signed URL controls.

## Compromised provider secret
Disable integration, rotate secret, inspect webhook/outbox activity, reauthorize least privilege, update vendor register.

## Incorrect service schedule
Publish explicit correction from canonical record, update structured data and drafts, notify affected members, record why propagation failed.
