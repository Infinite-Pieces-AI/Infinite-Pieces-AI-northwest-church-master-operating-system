# Security and Privacy Incident Response Plan

1. **Detect and triage:** open restricted incident record; classify severity and affected domains.
2. **Protect people first:** use emergency/safeguarding procedures where applicable.
3. **Contain:** revoke sessions/keys, disable integrations, isolate deployment, preserve evidence.
4. **Assess:** identify records, users, vendors, time window, access path, and legal/contractual obligations.
5. **Eradicate and recover:** patch, rotate, restore, validate RLS, monitor.
6. **Communicate:** approved leadership/counsel notices; never speculate publicly.
7. **Learn:** root cause, control changes, owner, due date, verification.

Do not store raw secrets or unnecessary sensitive row contents in the incident ticket. Maintain an offline contact tree and account recovery register.
